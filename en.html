Statok Privacy Policy

Effective: October 16, 2026. Contact: sandul.dev@gmail.com.

Statok is a personal finance tracker that works entirely on your phone.

  • We collect no data. No server, no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no internet access; nothing is sent to us or to third parties.
  • Stored on the device only: accounts, transactions, budgets, tags, payments, settings, and the lock PIN as a salted hash (never sent anywhere or included in backups). Deleted with the app or via the system "Clear data".
  • Backups: manual and automatic backups are written only where you choose (via the system file picker); we have no access. Backups are not encrypted. Android's own backup (if enabled) may store the database and settings in your Google account under Google's terms; lock settings are excluded.
  • Permissions: notifications (payment reminders, can be turned off); biometric checks are performed by Android, the app only receives the result.
  • EU users (GDPR): the developer processes no personal data — your data never leaves your phone, so we are neither its controller nor its processor under the GDPR; you manage it yourself on your device. You exercise your rights yourself: everything is visible and editable in the app, "Save backup to a file" and "Export to CSV" give you your data in a machine-readable form, and uninstalling the app or "Clear data" erases it completely. Android's own backup (if enabled) is handled by Google under its policy; we receive no data about your finances from them. Questions — to the contact email above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your country's data protection authority.
  • Children: not directed at children under 13; no data is collected.
  • Changes: a new version will be published at the same link with a new date.
